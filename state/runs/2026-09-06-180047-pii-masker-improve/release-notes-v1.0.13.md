Shut down gracefully and time out stalled connections. `cmd/pii-masker` handled no signals at all, so every deploy or restart cut off synchronous masking responses mid-flight and skipped `App.Close()`, leaving the retention sweeper stopped only by process death, and the `http.Server` had neither `ReadHeaderTimeout` nor `IdleTimeout`, so a connection that dribbled out its headers or an idle keep-alive connection could pin a goroutine indefinitely (Slowloris). `App.Run(ctx)`/`App.Serve(ctx, ln)` now stop accepting when the context is cancelled and `Shutdown` drains in-flight requests for up to `PII_MASKER_SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` seconds (default 45, deliberately longer than the 30s upstream timeout) before the background workers are stopped, and `main` wires that to `signal.NotifyContext` for SIGINT/SIGTERM. The header and idle guards are configurable through `PII_MASKER_READ_HEADER_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (default 15) and `PII_MASKER_IDLE_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (default 60), while `ReadTimeout`/`WriteTimeout` are intentionally left unset so 50MB uploads and slow inference responses are not severed; a value of zero or less falls back to the default rather than disabling the guard. Verified with three new `internal/app` tests (the listener closes after cancellation, an in-flight request whose body is only half-written through an `io.Pipe` still gets a complete response after cancellation, and a raw connection that never finishes its headers is dropped within 250ms) and three new `internal/config` tests (defaults, environment overrides, and fallback on zero, negative, and non-integer values); `gofmt -l` (no output), `go vet ./...`, `go build ./...`, `go test -count=1 ./...` and `go test -race -count=1 ./...` all pass, `-race -count=5` showed no flakiness, and temporarily reverting the `ReadHeaderTimeout` setting and `Shutdown` (to `Close`) confirmed the two new tests actually fail without the fix. The README documents the shutdown behaviour and the three new environment variables.
