release: ReSSO v0.9.88

The UserInfo POST endpoint lacked the 1 MiB form-body limit used by the
other protocol POST handlers. It now rejects oversized encoded forms with
400 invalid_request before parsing, for both header and body tokens and
fixed-length and chunked transfers. Exactly 1 MiB remains accepted.
Stamps the version in the Makefile, console package metadata and both
compose files, and documents the boundary and rollback behavior.
